How I work

I start every project with research: understanding the audience, the competition and what people actually search for. From there I fix the technical basics first, such as crawl errors, site speed and indexing, then optimize pages and content around search intent and build backlinks through outreach and guest posting. I track results in Google Analytics and Search Console, report them monthly in plain language, and adjust the plan based on what the data shows. Since starting in 2024, I've worked across a travel company, an IT firm and growth-focused teams, and I keep my methods current by following algorithm updates and testing new ideas. I stick to ethical, white-hat practices, because rankings that last matter more than quick wins.
